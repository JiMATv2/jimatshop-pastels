# JimatShop-Pastels User Guide: Shopper Edition

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **JimatShop-Pastels**, your localized mobile marketplace. This app allows you to find nearby shops, participate in flash sales, and earn JimatShop Coins for every purchase.

## 2. Getting Started
*   **Sign In**: Open the app and use the Login screen to access your account.
*   **Home Dashboard**: Your central hub for finding campaigns, browsing categories, and viewing featured products.

## 3. Finding Products
*   **Semantic Search**: Use the search bar for smart, intent-based results (e.g., "healthy snacks" or "nearest convenience store").
*   **Nearby Shops**: View the **Map** or the **Shops** list to find merchants closest to your current location.
*   **Campaigns & Flash Sales**: Keep an eye on the "Campaigns" section on the home screen for limited-time discounts.

## 4. The Shopping Experience
*   **Product Details**: Click on any product to see its description, price, and images.
*   **Add to Cart**: Collect items from multiple vendors. Manage your quantities in the **Cart** view.
*   **Checkout**:
    1.  Confirm your **Delivery Address** (uses real-time location search).
    2.  Apply **JimatShop Coins** to get discounts.
    3.  Select your payment method (integrated with Billplz for secure online banking).

## 5. Managing Your Orders
*   **Order Tracking**: Once purchased, go to **My Orders** to see the real-time status of your delivery.
*   **Order Details**: View your digital receipt and check for shipping updates or driver information.

## 6. JimatShop Loyalty Program
*   **Earning Coins**: Every successful checkout earns you JimatShop Coins.
*   **Spendable Balance**: View your current balance and transaction history in the **JimatShop Coins** menu. Use these coins at checkout for direct savings.

## 7. Refunds & Support
*   **Requesting a Refund**: If there is an issue with your order, navigate to the Order Details and click **Request Refund**.
*   **Evidence Upload**: You can take and upload photos of damaged or incorrect items directly through the app to support your claim.
